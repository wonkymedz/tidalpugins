Honest progress for segmented (lossy) downloads; strip illegal filename characters (v1.0.2)

- The client's DASH fetcher adds each segment's length to progress.total, so
  percentages jumped backwards and ETAs were nonsense for LOW/HIGH downloads.
  TiDLoad now detects a growing total (it only ever grows for segments) and shows
  "12.4 MB downloaded - segmented stream" with an indeterminate bar instead of a
  percentage/ETA. Single-stream FLAC downloads are unchanged, and the flag is
  cleared once the entry finishes.
- Filename sanitising now removes illegal characters instead of replacing them
  with "_": AC/DC -> ACDC, "Bad: Name?" -> "Bad Name". Leading/trailing dots are
  trimmed before and after sanitising so no segment can start with a dot.
- Also documented why lossy downloads are slower (many sequential segment
  requests + a post-download remux pass vs one streamed FLAC request).
- Tests: detectSegmented/progressPercent (core) + two engine tests proving a
  growing total is flagged and a fixed total is not; template tests updated for
  the stripping behaviour (156 total).
